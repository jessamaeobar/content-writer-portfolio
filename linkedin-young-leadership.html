LinkedIn: Young Leadership Is a Present-Tense Resource

CIVIC ENGAGEMENT & PUBLIC AWARENESS

Project Brief

A LinkedIn advocacy piece examining how institutions think about young leadership and challenging the idea that young people are only future leaders. The piece positions young perspectives as valuable to decision-making today.

Writing Approach

The writing uses a professional but accessible tone, beginning with a familiar phrase and questioning the assumptions behind it. It builds toward a broader institutional question about meaningful participation and the value of new voices.

Sample

What if “young leaders” stopped meaning “leaders someday”?

We use the phrase so often that it can become a way of putting young people permanently on the horizon.

Someday, they’ll lead.

Someday, they’ll shape policy.

Someday, their ideas will matter.

But young people are already living with the consequences of today’s decisions.

They’re entering the housing market. Starting careers. Building businesses. Caring for families. Organizing communities. Navigating institutions that weren’t necessarily designed with their generation in mind.

They don’t need to wait for a certain age, title, or résumé before their perspective becomes valuable.

The better question is:

Are our institutions creating meaningful ways for the people who will live with tomorrow’s decisions to help shape them today?

Young leadership isn’t a future pipeline.

It’s a present-tense resource.

And when institutions make room for new voices, they don’t simply give young people an opportunity to participate.

They give themselves a chance to see old problems differently.
