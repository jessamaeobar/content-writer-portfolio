The Story Behind Everlane Roots

CREATIVE CONTENT WRITING

Project Brief

This brand story sample was created for Everlane Roots, a sustainable lifestyle brand, to demonstrate my ability to write engaging, emotional, and brand-focused content that connects with audiences through storytelling.

Small Choices. Meaningful Change.

Every journey begins with a simple choice.

For Everlane Roots, it started with the belief that everyday products can have a greater purpose. A thoughtful purchase, a conscious decision, or a small change in daily habits can create a positive impact over time.

The brand was built around a simple idea: sustainability should feel accessible, practical, and meaningful—not complicated.

Every product reflects a commitment to quality, mindful design, and responsible choices. From carefully selecting materials to creating products made to last, Everlane Roots helps people make choices that are better for themselves and the world around them.

Because sustainability is not about being perfect.

It is about making better choices, one step at a time.

At Everlane Roots, every product carries a reminder:

Small actions can grow into meaningful change.
