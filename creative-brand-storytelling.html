The Things We Keep

CREATIVE CONTENT WRITING

Project Brief

This brand story sample was created for Everlane Roots, a sustainable lifestyle brand, to demonstrate my ability to write engaging, emotional, and brand-focused content that connects with audiences through storytelling.

Small Choices. Meaningful Change.

There is a moment, often so ordinary we barely notice it, when something we own reaches the end of its useful life.

A worn edge. A cracked handle. A shirt that has finally lost its shape.

And then comes the familiar question: Do I replace it, or do I keep it?

Replacing something has never been easier. When an everyday item wears out, there is always something newer waiting—faster, cheaper, more convenient. Somewhere along the way, we stopped asking how long something could stay with us and started asking what could replace it.

Everlane Roots began with a different thought:

What if we made everyday things worth keeping?

Not perfect things. Not products designed to make anyone feel guilty about what they buy. Just well-considered essentials made with the idea that what we choose to bring home should earn its place there.

That idea shapes everything Everlane Roots creates.

We look closely at the details that are easy to overlook: how something feels in your hands, how it fits into an ordinary routine, how it holds up after months of use, and whether you will still want it long after the novelty has worn off.

Because sustainability, to us, does not begin with perfection.

It begins with attention.

We pay attention to what we buy, what we already have, and the difference between wanting something new and needing something better.

That is why Everlane Roots takes a practical approach to responsible living. We believe more sustainable choices should fit into real lives—not require a complete reinvention of them.

You do not have to change everything at once.

Sometimes it is simply choosing the thing that lasts a little longer. Repairing instead of replacing. Buying with intention. Reaching for the familiar object that has become part of your routine because, somewhere along the way, it became yours.

These choices may seem small. But they are where change begins.

Everlane Roots exists to help us choose with intention—and create things worth keeping.

Because what we choose to keep says something about how we choose to live.
