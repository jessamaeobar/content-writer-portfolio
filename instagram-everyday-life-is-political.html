Instagram: Everyday Life Is Political

CIVIC ENGAGEMENT & PUBLIC AWARENESS

Project Brief

A short-form Instagram advocacy piece designed to make civic engagement feel relevant to everyday life. The piece connects familiar experiences such as rent, commuting, housing, libraries, and neighborhood conditions to the public decisions and priorities that shape them.

Writing Approach

The piece uses a conversational, accessible tone to connect everyday experiences with larger civic issues. It moves from familiar situations to a simple question about who makes decisions, ending with an invitation for readers to reflect on their own experiences.

Sample

You don’t have to love politics to care about your life.

Your rent. Your commute. Your neighborhood. The cost of your first apartment. Whether there’s a library you can actually get to after work.

Most of us don’t experience those things as “civic issues.”

We experience them as Tuesday.

But behind everyday life are decisions about budgets, policies, infrastructure, and priorities—and people deciding what gets attention.

Civic engagement can start much smaller than a political debate.

It can start with:

Wait. Who decides this?

Then ask. Learn. Show up. Bring someone with you.

Because paying attention is a form of participation, too.

What’s something in your everyday life you’ve realized is more political than you thought?
