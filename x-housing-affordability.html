Housing affordability can choose your future before you do.

It can decide whether you take the job across the country.

Whether you move closer to family.

Whether you live alone—or with three roommates.

Whether you save, start a business, go back to school, or stay put because moving is simply too expensive.

That’s why housing isn’t only about where people live.

It’s about how much freedom they have to decide what comes next.

What would you do differently if housing cost less?
